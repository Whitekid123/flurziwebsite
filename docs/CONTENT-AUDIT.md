## Design correction

Junca's live opening and scrolled content were reviewed in Chrome again. Reference captures:
- `screenshots/references/junca-clean-hero.png`
- `screenshots/references/junca-clean-content.png`

Apply the restraint: a direct company statement, generous spacing, a single typeface, simple navigation, work-led imagery, and dedicated pages. Keep motion focused on imagery. The earlier portal, repeated giant product names, decorative counters, serif slogan treatment and oversized footer wordmark are not part of the active design.

Flurzi's own product imagery and brand identities remain. No Junca assets, testimonials, client logos or company claims were copied.

## Current limits

- Contact prepares a draft in the visitor's email application; no backend delivery is represented.
- Upcoming projects have no invented launch dates.
- Product screenshots remain interim visual assets. User-supplied footage can be connected through `lib/media.ts`.
- No fabricated team, awards, company metrics, customer logos or additional services were added to fill pages.
