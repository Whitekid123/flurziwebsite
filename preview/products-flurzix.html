THE PLATFORM

Shoppers, independent sellers, retailers and merchants.

FlurziX is a commerce initiative for shoppers and businesses, with trust at the centre of its intended experience. Its development direction brings together verification, inspection information and traceability to help people make better-informed trading decisions. These capabilities are planned and are not being presented as operational.

CURRENT POSITION

FlurziX is under development. Verification, inspection and traceability are intended capabilities; the detailed operating model, commercial terms and launch timing are not yet confirmed.

Still in development.

Planned product direction. These features are not being advertised as available today.

IN DEVELOPMENT / 01

Trust & verification

Planned verification workflows are intended to help people assess who they are dealing with. Verification criteria and service scope remain under development.

IN DEVELOPMENT / 02

Inspection information

Inspection-related workflows form part of the intended product concept. Coverage, providers and the inspection process will be confirmed before availability.

IN DEVELOPMENT / 03

Traceability

The product direction includes traceable information and records to support transparency in commerce. No certification, guarantee or specific tracking technology is being claimed.
