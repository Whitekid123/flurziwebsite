THE PLATFORM

Players seeking accessible gaming and interactive entertainment.

Previously known as PlayVault, FlurziRush is being developed for accessible gaming across web and mobile. The direction includes younger audiences as well as a broader player community, with a focus on discovery and personalisation.

FlurziRush is under development. The initial launch is not intended to pay users cash for playing. The PlayVault identity was renamed FlurziRush in October 2026.
