Corporate website enquiries

Online enquiries are available only when email delivery is configured. When you submit the enabled form, your name, email address, optional organisation, selected topic and message are sent to Flurzi through its email delivery provider, Resend. Cloudflare Turnstile checks submissions for automated abuse. The corporate application does not store a separate enquiry database; messages are handled in the receiving inbox and by the delivery providers. Quality testing applicants may email their name, contact details, available test devices, relevant experience or interests, and general availability for consideration and communication about testing opportunities. Do not include passwords, payment details, identity documents or sensitive worker records.

Optional analytics and your choice

Where configured, Google Analytics measures visits to known corporate pages, platform access clicks, app download clicks and successfully submitted enquiry forms. It loads only after you accept optional analytics. The site does not pass form contents, email addresses or URL query strings to its custom analytics events. Google may process technical information such as IP addresses and device information.

Your analytics choice is stored in this browser. You can decline or withdraw using Analytics preferences in the footer. Declining does not prevent access to the website. Advertising personalisation and Google signals are disabled by this implementation. Analytics is not active when no measurement property has been configured.

Who this applies to

This policy applies to visitors, product users, prospective customers, employers, workers, readers, viewers, support contacts and anyone who communicates with Flurzi through this website or one of our platforms.

Information we may collect

The information involved depends on the platform and the features actually available. It may include contact and account details, organisation information, support messages, billing records where applicable, device and security logs, and product usage information. Workforce tools may involve employer records and worker documents. EndNovel may involve reading, audio, library and preference information. Descriptions of platforms in development do not mean their planned data collection or financial integrations are active.

How we use information

We use information to provide and improve our products, respond to enquiries, manage accounts, support product access, maintain security, process payments where applicable, send service messages, keep operational records and comply with legal obligations.

Lawful basis

Depending on the context, we may process personal information because it is necessary for a contract, because we have a legitimate interest in operating and improving our services, because we must comply with a legal obligation, because you gave consent, or because we need to take steps before entering into a contract.

Sharing information

We may share information with trusted service providers who support hosting, payment processing, communications, security, support and product operations. We do not sell personal information. Where a provider processes information for us, we expect it to act only on agreed instructions and protect the data appropriately.

Retention

Website enquiries and quality testing applications are retained for up to 12 months after the last meaningful interaction, unless a longer period is necessary for a documented legal or business reason. Other personal information is kept only as long as reasonably needed for its purpose, including account support, product records, security, legal, tax, audit and dispute-resolution requirements. Retention periods may differ between products because the data and obligations are different.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law, individuals may have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict, object to processing, request portability and complain to the Information Commissioner's Office. For current enquiry options and the registered postal address, visit the Contact page.
