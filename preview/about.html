OUR MISSION

Technology that
opens possibilities.

To develop accessible, intelligent and dependable technology that empowers individuals, businesses and organisations to overcome limitations, simplify complex processes and unlock new opportunities.

OUR VISION

Built here.
Thinking further.

To establish FLURZIHQ as a globally recognised technology headquarters delivering innovative digital products and solutions across e-commerce, financial technology, workforce management, entertainment and emerging technologies.

What we are
working towards.

Our capabilities are developing through the needs of our platforms. These are areas of focus, rather than claims of completed services across the whole portfolio.

Business & workforce

Tools for organisations, people and commerce. Flurzi360 has an available Version 1 and a broader workforce programme in development. FlurziX explores trust, verification, inspection and traceability in commerce.

Culture & entertainment

Stories people return to and new ways to play and watch. EndNovel is operational; FlurziRush and Flurzi Cinema are in development.

Financial commitments

A structured approach to savings and financial commitments through Flurzi Commit, with financial integrations pending.

Intelligent technology

Intelligent technology systems and supporting infrastructure through the development of Flurzi Cortex.
